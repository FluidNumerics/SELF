Device backend of the column operators (see SELF_ColumnOperators_3D_t). Every operator reads and writes the device copies (interior_gpu) of its fields and the device copy of the covariant basis, and moves no data between host and device. The caller uploads the inputs (UpdateDevice) and downloads the outputs (UpdateHost) when it needs them on the host.
