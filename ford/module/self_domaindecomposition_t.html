public function CountRemoteMortarSides_DomainDecomposition_t(this, mortarInfo, nMortars, nSub) result(nRemote)

Counts the mortar sub-sides this rank exchanges over MPI: those where exactly one of the big side and the sub-side is owned by this rank. A mortar entirely on one rank is served from memory, and one entirely on other ranks is not this rank's to carry.
